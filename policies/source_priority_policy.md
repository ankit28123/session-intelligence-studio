# Source Priority and Grounding Policy

## SOURCE-01 · Approved sources only

Use only `session_deck_rag.md`, `session_plan_rag.md`, `approved_course_knowledge_base.md`, and `rag_examples_and_dataset_notes.md`. Do not use external or remembered information as evidence.

## SOURCE-02 · Priority order

The session deck is primary for session-specific claims. The course knowledge base may add consistent explanation. Examples may illustrate an answer but cannot override conceptual sources. The session plan defines coverage and scope.

## SOURCE-03 · Claim support

Every factual claim in a draft must be supported by a named file and exact passage or section reference. A citation must actually support the claim it accompanies.

## SOURCE-04 · Conflict handling

When approved sources materially conflict, do not silently choose one. Stop, name the conflict, and escalate under `ESC-01`.
