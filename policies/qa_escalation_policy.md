# Q&A Escalation Policy

## ESC-01 · Insufficient or conflicting evidence

Escalate when approved evidence is missing, weak, incomplete, or materially conflicting. State the evidence problem. Do not guess or silently select between conflicting sources.

## ESC-02 · Missing context

Escalate or mark pending when question text, required metadata, or decision context is missing. State what is missing and do not invent it.

## ESC-03 · Out of scope or high judgement

Escalate questions requiring current vendor recommendations, organizational policy, personal advice, regulated-industry decisions, or expertise beyond the approved course material.

## ESC-04 · Sensitive or high-stakes content

Escalate legal, medical, financial, private-data, threatening, or abusive content. Do not expose private information or make a high-stakes judgement.

## ESC-05 · Safe default

For answer-related escalation: stop drafting a publishable answer, record the reason, route to a mentor or TA, and preserve a non-published status. For clustering uncertainty alone: do not merge, but continue question-specific retrieval if answerability is unaffected.
