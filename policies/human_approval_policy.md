# Human Approval and Publication Policy

## APPROVAL-01 · Required human gate

Every learner-facing answer requires an explicit mentor or TA action: approve as-is, edit and approve, reject, or escalate. The agent cannot approve its own output or infer approval from conversational language.

## APPROVAL-02 · Exact-version approval

Approval applies only to the reviewed question ID and exact draft version. Editing or regenerating a draft invalidates previous approval and returns the new version to pending.

## APPROVAL-03 · Publication validation

Before publication, verify question ID, reviewer ID, approval ID, approval status, and draft version. Missing, expired, rejected, edited, unapproved, or version-mismatched records must result in no publication.

## APPROVAL-04 · Permitted final states

Each question must end as pending, approved and published, rejected, or escalated. Publication is never a default state.
