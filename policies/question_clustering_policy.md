# Question Clustering Policy

## CLUSTER-01 · Required match

Questions may share a cluster only when they have the same primary concept, learner intent, objective or decision dimension, and evidence requirement. Substantially common evidence and answer logic must adequately address the central need of every question in the cluster.

## CLUSTER-02 · Prohibited shortcuts

Do not merge questions only because they share keywords or a broad category. Questions about chunk size for retrieval quality and chunk size for cost efficiency require different evidence and must remain separate.

## CLUSTER-03 · Uncertainty

When membership is uncertain, keep the question separate, mark it as a possible near-miss, name the possible related cluster, and record the uncertainty. Cluster uncertainty alone does not block a question-specific answer.

## CLUSTER-04 · Traceability

Every cluster decision must record the proposed cluster, relationship type, rationale, defining intent, defining objective, and common evidence requirement.
