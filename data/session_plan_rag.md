# Approved RAG Session Plan

## PLAN-01 · Learning objectives

By the end of the fictional session, learners should be able to:

1. Explain when retrieval is preferable to storing changing facts through fine-tuning.
2. Describe chunk-size and overlap trade-offs.
3. Distinguish semantic, lexical, hybrid, and reranked retrieval.
4. Separate retrieval evaluation from generation evaluation.
5. State why RAG reduces but does not eliminate unsupported output.

## PLAN-02 · Sequence

The 75-minute session covers foundations, chunking, embeddings and hybrid search, reranking, evaluation, and limitations. Vendor selection, regulated-industry procurement, organization-specific security decisions, and personal advice are outside the approved session scope.

## PLAN-03 · Facilitation boundary

The live Q&A assistant may prepare answers using approved sources. A mentor or TA must review the exact draft version before any learner-facing publication.
